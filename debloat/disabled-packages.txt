# Packages the launcher can switch off with `pm disable-user --user 0`
# (Settings > Home and apps > Clean up apps > Clean up the TV), each with the reason.
# Nothing here is switched off until you press the buttons on the TV and confirm.
# Undo from the TV ("Switch back on what I switched off") or with ./restore.sh.
#
# Recommended: leftovers from the factory and the shop
com.google.android.feedback            Sends feedback and crash data to Google
com.google.android.partnersetup        Only used during first-time setup
com.google.android.onetimeinitializer  Runs once at setup
android.autoinstalls.config.JVC        The maker's retail auto-install settings
com.google.android.leanbacklauncher.partnercustomizer  Lets the maker add promoted rows to Google's home
fusion.android.tv.demo                 Shop demo mode
mediatek.factorymenu.ui                Technician menu
com.mediatek.testpq                    Factory picture test
com.mediatek.mtc.factory               Factory test service
com.mstar.android.tv.disclaimercustomization  First-run legal screens
com.google.android.sss.authbridge      Phone-to-TV setup helper
com.google.android.backdrop            Google's screensaver (the ambient screen replaces it)
com.google.android.syncadapters.calendar  Calendar sync, which a TV does not use
com.android.dynsystem                  A developer feature
#
# Careful, one at a time (test the TV after each)
com.mediatek.network                   Wi-Fi monitor
com.google.android.katniss             The Google app: voice search and Assistant (keep if you use the voice button)
#
# Only once this launcher is the home screen and working
com.google.android.tvlauncher          Google's home screen, where the ads come from
com.google.android.tvrecommendations   Google's recommendations for its home screen
#
# Never touched: Android and Google core services, TV inputs and the MediaTek TV services,
# Freeview Play background services, the keyboard, Cast, Downloader, the update service.
