Set up your TV

This page is served by the TV for as long as the setup screen is open there.

Enter the code from the TV

Govee lights

Not connected.

Get a key in the Govee Home app: Profile, then Settings (the cog), then Apply for API Key. Govee emails it to you. It is stored on the TV and only ever sent to Govee.

Show search (TMDB)

A free key from themoviedb.org (Settings, API) lets the TV search shows and films and say which of your apps carry them. It shows free or subscription, and rent or buy, but not prices.

Guest Wi-Fi code

Build a code that guests scan to join. The preview updates as you type. Save it to show the code on the TV (Settings, Network and phone, Guest Wi-Fi). The password stays on the TV and in the code.

Weather town

Leave this empty to use the TV's own location.

Wallpapers

Photos are scaled to fit the TV. Landscape photos look best.

    Apps to switch off

    Tick the apps you don't want. Nothing happens yet: the choice is sent to the TV, and you then switch them off from the TV itself (Settings, Home and apps, Clean up apps) after a final confirmation. Switching off is reversible. Apps the TV needs, and the ones you use, are locked.
